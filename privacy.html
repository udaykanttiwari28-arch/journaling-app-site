Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Overview

My Little Space is a personal journaling and habit tracking app. Your privacy is fundamental to our design. The app does not collect or share your personal information. Your data is stored locally on your device, except when you choose to use Apple's optional iCloud backup or speech recognition services as described below.

Data Storage

By default, your data is stored on your device:

  • Journal entries (text, photos, videos, audio) are saved to your device's local storage.
  • Habits and completions are stored in a local database on your device.
  • The app has no developer-operated cloud backend, analytics service, or third-party data processor. Data leaves the device only when you choose Apple's iCloud backup or speech recognition services described below.

Encryption

Journal text and titles, habit titles, photos, videos, and audio recordings are encrypted at the application level using AES-256-GCM through Apple's CryptoKit framework. Other local database values, including habit completion records, are protected by iOS data protection.

iCloud Backup

The app offers an optional iCloud Drive backup feature. If you enable it:

  • Your app data, including app-encrypted content and habit completion records, is copied to your personal iCloud Drive storage.
  • The backup is protected by Apple's iCloud security in transit and in storage.
  • Only you can access your iCloud Drive backups through your Apple ID.
  • This feature is entirely optional and disabled by default.

Face ID / Touch ID

The app uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework to protect access to your journal. Biometric data (Face ID or Touch ID) is processed entirely by the iOS operating system. The app never accesses, stores, or transmits any biometric data.

HealthKit

The app can optionally read steps, sleep, water, active calories, and workouts from Apple Health to automatically track health-related habits. If you grant permission:

  • Health data is read only for the habit metrics you authorize. My Little Space never writes data to Apple Health.
  • Values used to record habit completions are stored in the local database under iOS data protection and may be included in your optional iCloud backup.
  • Health data is not included in journal PDF exports and is never shared with third parties.
  • You can manage access at any time in the Apple Health app by tapping your profile picture, then Apps and Services > My Little Space.

Camera, Photos & Microphone

The app requests access to your camera, photo library, and microphone to let you attach media to journal entries and use voice dictation. Captured media is stored locally with app-level encryption and may be included in your optional iCloud backup. It is not shared with third parties.

Speech Recognition

The app uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework for voice-to-text dictation. When available, on-device recognition is used. Transcribed text stays in the app and is not sent to any external service beyond Apple's standard speech recognition processing.

Data Collection

My Little Space does not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Crash reports (beyond what Apple collects through standard iOS mechanisms with your consent)

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms. There are no ads.

Data Deletion

You can delete any journal entry or habit at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all local data permanently. If you have iCloud backups enabled, you can delete backups from your iCloud Drive.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
